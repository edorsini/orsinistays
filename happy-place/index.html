Your Cap Cana escape

Polished design with resort-style amenities

Happy Place is a stylish one-bedroom apartment in a gated Cap Cana community. Enjoy a comfortable king bed, a full kitchen, fast Wi-Fi, and a private balcony overlooking the pool.

The building adds the conveniences of an elevator, underground parking, a modern gym, and inviting shared spaces. Juanillo Beach, Cap Cana Marina, golf, dining, and area attractions are all within easy reach.
