Local recommendations worth saving

A head start on the beaches, restaurants, coffee stops, and everyday essentials we recommend to our own friends and family.

🍽️ Eat, Drink & Explore

🛍️

Galerías Puntacana

Shopping mall in The Village with boutiques, restaurants, and local stores.

🛒

Supermercado Nacional

Well-stocked grocery in BlueMall Puntacana — produce, meat, wine, bakery & essentials.

🍺

Cervecería Punta Cana

Craft beer and casual bites in Puntacana Village — good for relaxed evenings.

🛍️

Blue Mall

Modern shopping & dining complex near the airport with international brands and local boutiques.

🌿

Ojos Indígenas Ecological Reserve

Nature park with trails and crystal-clear freshwater lagoons — swim and explore the forest.

🎭

Coco Bongo

High-energy show venue: live performances, acrobatics and nonstop entertainment.

🎢

Bávaro Adventure Park

Eco-park with ziplining, dune buggies, horseback riding and water attractions across 110 acres.

⚓

Cap Cana Marina

World-class marina with shops, restaurants, sport fishing and yacht charters.

💦

El Dorado Water Park

Family-friendly water park in Cap Cana with slides, pools and play areas.

🕳️

Imagine Cave

Nightclub inside natural caves — unique atmosphere with live DJs and dramatic chambers.

🧭

Scape Park

Eco-adventure park in Cap Cana — zip lines, caves, cenote swimming and the turquoise Hoyo Azul.

🏖️ Beaches

Best beaches near our stays
🏝️

Playa Blanca

White sand, calm turquoise waters and beachfront dining — great for swimming and relaxing.

🌅

API Beach

Relaxed beach club at Cap Cana Marina — calm waters, seafood and sunset vibes.

🌊

Uvero Alto

Quieter stretch north of Punta Cana with golden sand and a more tranquil feel.

🏄

Macao Beach

Famous public beach with wide sands, local food shacks and surfable waves.

🌴

Arena Gorda

Long, wide beach popular for swimming and close to many all-inclusive resorts.

🍹

Juanillo Beach

Pristine white sand in Cap Cana with calm waters and beachfront restaurants.

🐠

Cabeza de Toro Beach

Palm-lined and picturesque — great snorkeling and fishing excursions.

🏖️

El Cortecito

Vibrant local beach in Bávaro with colorful shops, bars and casual restaurants.

⭐

Playa Bávaro

The iconic Punta Cana beach — miles of white sand, turquoise water and lively beach life.
