Where to stay near Punta Cana Airport (PUJ)

By Orsini Stays · Updated October 16, 2026

Staying near Punta Cana International Airport can make the first and last day of a trip feel much easier—especially with a late flight, a short stay, children, or a morning departure. The right choice is not only about the fewest minutes from the terminal; it is about choosing a base that still matches how you want to spend the rest of the trip.

For the shortest airport ride

Punta Cana Village Dreamcatcher is about five minutes from PUJ. This two-bedroom home works particularly well for a quick getaway, a late arrival, or a small group that wants to settle in fast. It has a pool view, covered balcony, kitchen, dedicated workspace, and two parking spaces. Restaurants, groceries, and local shops are nearby, while Playa Blanca is about seven minutes away by car.

For a Cap Cana-centered stay

Cap Cana Happy Place is about 17 minutes from PUJ. It is the better match if your priorities are a one-bedroom retreat with a private balcony, shared pool and gym, elevator access, and easy reach of Juanillo Beach, the marina, golf, and Cap Cana attractions. You will spend a little longer on the arrival transfer, but the base may be a better fit for your plans once you unpack.

A simple way to decide

  • One or two nights, a late arrival, or an early flight: Put Punta Cana Village high on your list.
  • A small family, friends, or remote-work needs: Dreamcatcher’s two bedrooms and workspace are useful practical advantages.
  • A couple’s trip with Cap Cana on the itinerary: Happy Place gives you a focused one-bedroom home base near Juanillo Beach.
  • Several beach days in different areas: Choose based on the beaches and activities you expect to visit most—not airport time alone.

Plan the transfer, too

Drive times are approximate and can change with traffic and the exact pickup point. Confirm your transportation plan, check-in timing, luggage needs, and current road conditions before travel.
